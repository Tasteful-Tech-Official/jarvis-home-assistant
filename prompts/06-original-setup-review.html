Prompt 06 - Verify the existing Mac-based stack (ChatGPT Work)

Before making changes, inspect my actual hardware, operating system, available memory, Home Assistant address and existing configuration. Ask me for any details you cannot verify.

Audit my existing local AI voice assistant without reinstalling anything. The video used Ollama on an Apple Silicon Mac, Home Assistant Assist, Wyoming MLX Whisper and Piper, but my actual hardware and services may differ. Do not assume what is installed. Home Assistant may be on another device.

Identify the actual Ollama model and reachable IP, confirm ports and process startup for the installed language model and speech services, inspect the Wyoming integrations and the selected Assist pipeline. Test a typed conversation, a phone voice request and a browser wake-word request if available.

Record observed evidence, current bottlenecks, service restart commands and what survives a system reboot. Do not reveal local secrets or change network/firewall permissions. Report what is confirmed vs unknown and provide a safe plan for any fixes.
